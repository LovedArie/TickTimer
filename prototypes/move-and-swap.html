Try this

  1. Swap: drag Plan A (Fri 10 AM) and drop it on Plan B (1 PM). They trade start times; each keeps its length.
  2. Move to another day: switch to Week, drag Plan A into Saturday morning.
  3. Full slot: drop anything on Sat 2 PM (three blocks already there).
  4. The past: try to move Gym (Fri 8 AM), or drop something on Thursday.
  5. Tracked time: drag Email & admin (25m tracked) to Saturday: refused. Later today works. Standup call is being timed right now, so it can't move at all.
  6. Recurring: drag Friday's LOG635 - TP to Saturday. Only that one moves, and Friday does not come back. Then press +30 min on the rule: the moved one stays put, next Friday's follows.
  7. Phone: switch to Phone. Hold a block, then tap where it goes. Try both "Hold a block" settings.
